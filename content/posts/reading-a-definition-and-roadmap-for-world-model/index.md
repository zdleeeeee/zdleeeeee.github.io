Original paper: [A Definition and Roadmap for World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06401)

# Core Content

To give a comprehensive perspective and road map for world models, the paper provides:

- A **scientific definition** of a world model.
- A **two-dimensional taxonomy** to classify existing systems.
- A **staged roadmap** outlining the necessary steps to develop next-generation, foundation-scale world models capable of enabling Physical AGI (Artificial General Intelligence).
